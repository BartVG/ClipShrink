# User guide

ClipShrink works through four numbered steps in the sidebar. This guide follows the same order, so you can read it alongside the app.

## The steps

1. **Find videos.** Pick a folder; ClipShrink lists the H.264 videos in it and its subfolders. Nothing is changed. See [Finding videos](finding-videos.md).
2. **See the saving.** Short test encodes of a few of your own files show how much space you would save, before anything is converted. See [See the saving](estimates.md).
3. **Convert.** Each video is converted, then measured against its original. You can pause, and you can reopen the app after a crash. See [Converting](converting.md).
4. **Compare and clean up.** Play an original beside its conversion, and optionally send originals to the Recycle Bin. See [Compare and clean up](compare-and-clean-up.md).

The app opens on a Welcome screen, which you can turn off. **Settings** is at the bottom of the sidebar, with **About ClipShrink** beside the settings on the same page. To leave it, click **Back** or the step you are on in the left pane. ClipShrink speaks seven languages; pick yours at the top of [Settings](settings-and-data.md#language). When you finish step 4, exit the app or click **Start Over** to begin fresh. ClipShrink then forgets those files, and only the [reports](reports.md) remain.

Your originals are only read during conversion. Apart from the optional [GoPro LRV clean-up](finding-videos.md#gopro-lrv-files) of preview files, step 4 is the only place where your original videos are removed, and it is optional.
