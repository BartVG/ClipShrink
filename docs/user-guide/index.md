# User guide

ClipShrink works through four numbered steps in the sidebar, plus a reports page. This guide follows the same order, so you can read it alongside the app.

## The steps

1. **Find videos.** Pick a folder; ClipShrink lists the H.264 videos in it and its subfolders. Nothing is changed. See [Finding videos](finding-videos.md).
2. **See the saving.** Short test encodes of a few of your own files show how much space you would save, before anything is converted. See [Seeing the saving](estimates.md).
3. **Convert.** Each video is converted, then measured against its original. You can pause, and you can reopen the app after a crash. See [Converting](converting.md).
4. **Compare and clean up.** Play an original beside its conversion, and optionally send originals to the Recycle Bin. See [Compare and clean up](compare-and-clean-up.md).
5. **Reports.** Statistics and a report for every batch. See [Reports and statistics](reports.md).

Your originals are only read during conversion. Step 4 is the only place where anything is removed, and it is optional.
